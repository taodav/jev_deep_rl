## OCAtari

RAM field definitions and object geometry reference OCAtari's
[Pong](https://github.com/k4ntz/OC_Atari/blob/master/ocatari/ram/pong.py),
[Breakout](https://github.com/k4ntz/OC_Atari/blob/master/ocatari/ram/breakout.py),
[Ms. Pac-Man](https://github.com/k4ntz/OC_Atari/blob/master/ocatari/ram/mspacman.py),
and [Montezuma's Revenge](https://github.com/k4ntz/OC_Atari/blob/master/ocatari/ram/montezumarevenge.py)
decoders. Montezuma room templates in `jev_rl/montezuma_rooms.py` are adapted
from that project's geometry definitions.

Fetched 2026-09-19. Geometry is stored as data; no OCAtari runtime dependency.
